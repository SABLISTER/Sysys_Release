# ABCT AI Info-Sheet Workflow

Use `templates/abct_tech_ai_infosheet.yaml` for the June 22, 2026 ABCT
Technology Committee AI subcommittee info-sheet on ethical and practical
guidance for AI-enabled digital mental health.

## Generate the One-Page Handout

1. Open SystS with `python main.py`.
2. Open File -> Settings and load `templates/abct_tech_ai_infosheet.yaml`.
3. On the Search tab, review the generated question and search terms, then run
   Search.
4. Run Score & Filter. For a quick meeting packet, continue with Extract and
   Audit & Synthesize when time allows; the export also works from partial
   pipeline state.
5. Go to Report & Export and click `Export ABCT AI Info-Sheet...`.
6. Use `abct_tech_ai_infosheet.md` as the one-page handout. The same export
   directory includes PRISMA-trAIce files, a bibliography, a flow diagram, and
   any available synthesis or validation artifacts for traceability.

## Preset Focus

- Ethical guardrails: privacy, consent, transparency, accountability, bias, and
  fairness.
- Practical guidance: clinician oversight, workflow fit, risk management,
  training, documentation, and governance.
- Safety: adverse events, high-risk use cases, crisis escalation, and limits on
  autonomous support.
- Equity and access: digital divide, underserved populations, language support,
  disability access, and culturally responsive deployment.
- Committee actionability: evidence-backed statements, caution language, and
  agenda prompts for the June 22 meeting.
